What the waitlist collects

When you join, we collect your email address, selected interests, signup time, and your agreement to receive relevant updates. Your name and organisation are optional. We also record whether you came from our home or hardware page and a campaign label, if one is provided in the signup link.

How we use it

We use this information to manage the waitlist, understand interest in our products, and contact you about your selected interests. A signup does not create an order, guarantee availability, or commit you to a purchase. Signup numbers reflect registration order; invitations may depend on the product and its availability.

Where it is processed

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Our waitlist uses Cloudflare Workers and D1 to process and store submissions. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions for abuse. Spacemail processes automated signup confirmations sent from team@vernia.in. Your IP address is processed for security checks and short-lived rate limiting; our waitlist database does not store it. These providers may process information in locations outside your country.

Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate visits and page performance. If you allow optional analytics, we also record page paths, referring domains, campaign labels, country, device category, visible-page engagement, and whether a visit resulted in a signup. A random identifier in your browser's session storage links activity within one tab and expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Your choice is saved in local storage. Our Cloudflare database retains these optional events for 90 days; we also send the same limited events to our PostHog project in the United States for analysis. We do not send names, email addresses, form content, or full referring URLs to these analytics tools. Session recording and RB2B visitor identification are not enabled.

You can change your optional analytics choice below. We respect your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals for optional analytics. Script blockers and consent choices reduce measured coverage; some automated visits can still be counted. These figures are estimates, not identified customers.

Analytics preferences

Enable JavaScript to manage optional analytics. Optional analytics remain off without JavaScript.

Leaving the list or correcting information

We keep your signup while managing your waitlist participation and related updates, unless you ask us to remove it. Provider backups may retain deleted data for their normal backup period.
