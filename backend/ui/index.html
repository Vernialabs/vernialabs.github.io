What these numbers mean

Browser sessions are visits within one browser tab, ending after 30 minutes of inactivity. Engaged sessions have at least 10 seconds of visible page activity. Known bots are filtered, but automation can still imitate people. Consent choices and blockers reduce measured coverage.

PostHog receives the same minimal, consented events when connected. Names, email addresses, and form content are not sent. RB2B is not enabled; identifying visitors requires a separate integration and privacy review.
