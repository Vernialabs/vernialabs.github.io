+ BUILT TO OBSERVE THE WHOLE ACTION

More than
what a camera sees.

Visual context, scene depth, contact force, and grip position. Four complementary signals describe what happens during a demonstration.

01Fisheye cameraVISUAL CONTEXT
A wide view of the gripper and its surroundings, keeping the object and the wider scene in context.
02Depth cameraSPATIAL CONTEXT
Captures distance and scene geometry, adding a spatial dimension to the visual record of an interaction.
03Force sensorCONTACT FEEDBACK
Measures contact force during a grasp, adding information about the interaction that imagery alone cannot provide.
04Grip encoderGRIP POSITION
Tracks the gripper’s opening and closing, recording grip position as the demonstration unfolds.
05Device weightHANDHELD FORM
